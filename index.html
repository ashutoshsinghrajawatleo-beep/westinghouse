About Us

About Westing House Industries

Westing House Industries is a trusted manufacturer based in Bari Brahmana, Jammu, specialising in high-quality home appliances designed for Indian households.

Our water heater geysers, air coolers and fans are engineered to deliver durability, energy efficiency and dependable performance — without stretching the household budget. Every unit that leaves our factory is built on the same principle: appliances should simply work, year after year.

We believe in innovation, honest pricing and long-lasting product quality, backed by a customer support team that's easy to reach and quick to respond.

  • In-house manufacturing unit
  • 100% pre-dispatch testing
  • Pan-India dealer network
  • GST registered manufacturer
